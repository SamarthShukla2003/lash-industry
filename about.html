About Me

The artist behind the lashes

Hi! I'm Jane

I have always believed that when you feel beautiful on the outside, it ignites a confidence that radiates from within. With over 5 years of experience in the beauty industry, I've dedicated my career to mastering the delicate art of eyelash extensions and microblading.

My approach is deeply personalized. I don't believe in a "one size fits all" style. Every set of lashes is meticulously designed to complement your unique eye shape, natural lashes, and lifestyle.

When I'm not in the studio creating lash magic, you can find me exploring new coffee shops, traveling, or staying up-to-date with the latest beauty trends.
