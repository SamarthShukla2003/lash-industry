Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

General

How long do lash extensions last?
With proper care and maintenance, a full set of eyelash extensions can last anywhere from 4 to 6 weeks. However, because your natural lashes have a growth cycle, they will shed naturally, taking the extension with them. We recommend booking a refill every 2-3 weeks to keep your lashes looking full and beautiful.
Are lash extensions safe?
Yes, when applied by a licensed and trained professional using quality products, lash extensions are completely safe and do not damage your natural lashes.

Aftercare

How do I care for my lashes?
  • Avoid getting them wet for the first 24 hours.
  • Wash them daily with a lash-safe foaming cleanser.
  • Avoid oil-based makeup and skincare products around the eyes.
  • Brush them gently daily with a clean spoolie.
  • Do not pick or pull at your extensions.
Can I wear eye makeup with extensions?
Yes, you can wear oil-free makeup, such as oil-free eyeliner and eyeshadow. However, mascara is NOT recommended on volume sets as it collapses the fans. If you must wear mascara on classic lashes, only use a water-based formula designed specifically for extensions.
