Policies & Information

Please review our studio policies before booking

Cancellation Policy

Your appointments are very important to us. We understand that sometimes schedule adjustments are necessary; therefore, we respectfully request at least 24 hours notice for cancellations.

  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance will result in a charge equal to 50% of the reserved service amount.
  • "No-Shows" will be charged 100% of the reserved service amount.
  • Appointments made within the 24 hour period and need to cancel, the client then must cancel within 4 hours of appointment time or will result in a charge equal to 50% of the reserved service amount.

Late Arrivals

If you arrive late, we will simply limit the time of your service so the next client is not delayed. Please note that the full service fee still applies.

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment will be canceled and the cancellation fee will apply.

Payment & Deposits

A non-refundable $50 deposit is required to secure your booking. This deposit goes toward the total cost of your service. The remaining balance is due at the time of your appointment.

We accept all major credit cards, cash, and Zelle.

Refund Policy

We do not offer refunds on services rendered. If you have an allergic reaction, we will remove the lashes free of charge. If you are unhappy with your service, please contact us within 48 hours and we will do our best to make it right.
